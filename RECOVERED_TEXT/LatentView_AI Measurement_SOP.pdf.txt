LatentView SOP for AI Measurement & Tagging in PRM As LatentView makes the pivot to an AI-first company, there is a strong need to establish robust definitions for AI work, measurement & tagging mechanisms to seamlessly measure AI work at various levels and most importantly a clear governance model for AI project tracking. This SOP defines the various kinds of AI work that LatentView will deliver and how these projects will be seamlessly tagged and tracked in PRM for various reporting purposes. 1. Definition of Primary & Secondary AI Projects Primary AI: 1. Objective - This tag ensures that the project being executed is a Core AI Engagement. 2. Goal: To Reward True AI capability, strategy, "architectural-minded thinking". 3. Definition - The engagement exists because of AI. Every primary deliverable is an AI artifact: models, pipelines, agents and/or systems. The client is paying for an AI capability and removing AI collapses the scope of the project entirely. There are two sub-divisions under Primary AI – 1) Traditional; 2) Modern Primary AI - Traditional: Covers all AI work that is classical ML / Predictive in nature. If standalone model-building is the mandate, then the model is the core deliverable for the project. The following types of work will be covered in this category; S. SOP Executive Summary & Core Policy ● SOP Objective: To standardize the identification, categorization, validation and auditing of AI projects (Primary vs. Secondary) across all entities to ensure accurate external reporting, precise internal productivity tracking and fair incentive payouts for AI leaders. ● Scope: Applied to all new and existing opportunities logged in PRM with a winning probability of >=50% (to prevent SDE fatigue on low-probability pipeline deals). ● Ownership Matrix: ○ Initiator: Project Owner (PO) / SPOC as given in PRM ○ Reviewer & Approver: SDE ○ Auditor: Shared responsibility between SDE and Finance (during end-of-quarter reconciliations). 3. The PRM System Modifications (UI/UX Layer) A new mandatory section titled ‘AI Project Type Categorization’ to be introduced in the PRM opportunity/project creation wizard. Field Architecture & Validation Rules 1. AI Classification Dropdown: Options: Non-AI (Default), Primary AI - Traditional, Primary AI - Modern, Secondary AI. ○ System Trigger: If any of the above AI tags except Non-AI is selected, fields 2 and 3 become mandatory, and the submission route forces an SDE approval queue entry. 2. AI Capability Tag (Multi-select checkbox): Active only if AI is selected. Options include: GenAI / Agentic Frameworks, Data Science & ML, MLOps & LLMOps, Advanced Analytics/NLQ, AI-Assisted Development (final list to be confirmed by SDE when built into PRM) 3. System Guardrail: The project status cannot transition from "'Pipeline" to "Confirmed" until all three tracks (Finance, RMG, and SDE) have granted "Approved" status. Phase 3: SDE Review, Evaluation and Action ● Step 3.1: SDE receives an automated email/system notification: "New AI Project Approval Request: [Project Name] - [Client Name] - [Entity]". ● Step 3.2: An SDE reviewer evaluates the request within an SLA of 72 hours using the "Governing Classification Test" as described in the definitions section. ● Step 3.3: SDE takes one of three actions in PRM: 1. Approve: Tagging is verified; project proceeds. 2. Recategorize: SDE downgrades/upgrades the project (e.g., changing a requested Primary to Secondary or vice versa) with a mandatory system comment explaining why. 3. Reject / Clarify: Sent back to the PO for more detailed documentation. 4.
